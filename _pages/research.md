  My research focuses on **hardware security** and **microelectronics trust**, with particular emphasis on protecting commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) processors from malicious threats.

  I develop approaches using **side-channel analysis**, **electromagnetic monitoring**, and **secure coding techniques** to detect hardware Trojans and protect sensitive data on untrusted silicon. The work spans theoretical foundations through practical implementations for embedded systems and critical infrastructure.
