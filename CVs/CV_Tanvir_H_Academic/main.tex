\begin{rSection}{Research Interests}

My research develops attacks and defenses for trustworthy microelectronics across the hardware and software stack. I work on power and electromagnetic side-channel analysis, fault injection, hardware Trojan detection and mitigation, and intellectual-property protection, combining digital VLSI and ASIC design flows (RTL design, verification, and synthesis) with machine learning to build golden-chip-free, pre-deployment security evaluation and assurance techniques for integrated circuits.
\end{rSection}
