\begin{rSection}{Selected Projects}
\begin{itemize}

    \item \textbf{RTL Evaluation of Design-for-Security Scan \& PUF Primitives:} Modeled and verified Ring-Oscillator PUFs and Dynamically Obfuscated Scan Chains (DOSC) at RTL, then injected and simulated targeted modifications to characterize behavior at block and system level under directed stimulus \,---\, exercising scan-chain and authentication logic against specification.
    \item \textbf{Multi-Valued Logic Circuits for Hardware:} Designed and simulated multi-valued logic circuits, analyzing functional behavior and trade-offs; published at IEEE DCAS 2023.
    \item \textbf{GNRFET-Based 6T/7T SRAM Cells:} Performed transistor-level design and SPICE simulation of SRAM bit-cells, characterizing read/write behavior and stability across variants.
    \item \textbf{FPGA Hardware Teaching Platform:} Built Verilog/FPGA and ESP32 design modules and demos used in NSF-funded curricula, validating each on Artix-7 and Intel MAX10 boards.
\end{itemize}
