about_me: |
  I'm a PhD researcher in Electrical Engineering at the University of Kansas, working on **hardware security** and **microelectronics trust**. My research develops techniques to detect hardware Trojans and protect sensitive computation on commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) processors using side-channel analysis, electromagnetic monitoring, and secure encoding.

  ### Research focus
  - **Hardware security**: detecting and mitigating vulnerabilities in COTS ICs with formal verification and side-channel methods
  - **Secure hardware design**: attack-resilient architectures and cryptographic strategies for untrusted silicon
  - **Engineering education**: NSF-supported gamified modules that teach computer hardware fundamentals on FPGAs and embedded boards

  ### My Vision
  This platform serves as a dedicated space for my research activities in microelectronics, circuit design, and hardware security. I aim to share insights from my research and academic works, including research findings, project developments, and technical discussions. Occasionally, I will also explore philosophical perspectives and broader academic themes through blog posts.

  I share research notes, tutorials, and broader reflections on the [blog](/blog/). If you're interested in collaboration, please [get in touch](/contact/).
